SIR rules as FAQ

From ECI's 27 Oct 2025 instructions, the Delhi CEO FAQ and the Supreme Court judgment of 27 May 2026 (2026 INSC 564).

Quick filters:
Enumeration
  • BLO delivers pre-printed forms in duplicate and makes at least 3 visits to collect them.
  • The elector's copy gets an acknowledgement. Any adult family member can sign.
  • No document is collected at this stage.
  • BLO carries at least 30 blank Form 6 and declaration forms for new electors.
  • Online option: voters.eci.gov.in with OTP login and OTP e-sign. It works only if the roll name matches the Aadhaar name.
  • Electors give their own or a relative's entry from the last SIR roll (2002 in Delhi).
Forms not returned
  • BLO notes a probable reason after asking neighbours: absent, shifted, dead or duplicate.
  • Booth-wise lists with reasons go on notice boards at the Panchayat or urban body office and the Block Development Officer's office.
  • CEOs put the lists on their website. Parties' booth-level agents get them before the draft roll.
Draft roll and claims
  • Draft roll comes with a Form 5 notice, which also invites advance applications for 1 April, 1 July and 1 October 2026 qualifying dates.
  • About a month for claims and objections. Anyone missing from the draft roll files Form 6 with a declaration form.
Notices and documents
  • The ERO issues a notice only after the draft roll, and only to electors who could not be linked to the last SIR roll.
  • Born before 1 July 1987: a document for yourself.
  • Born 1 July 1987 to 2 December 2004: a document for yourself and one for a parent.
  • Born after 2 December 2004: documents for yourself, your father and your mother. If a parent is not Indian, their passport and visa from the time of your birth.
  • Born abroad: birth registration from the Indian Mission. Citizenship by registration or naturalisation: the certificate.
  • Aadhaar counts as identity proof only, not citizenship, and officers may verify it.

Indicative list (any one per person):

  1. Government or PSU employee ID or pension order
  2. Government, local body, bank, Post Office, LIC or PSU document issued before 1 July 1987
  3. Birth certificate
  4. Passport
  5. Matriculation or educational certificate
  6. Permanent residence certificate
  7. Forest Right Certificate
  8. OBC, SC, ST or other caste certificate
  9. National Register of Citizens, where it exists
  10. Family register
  11. Government land or house allotment certificate
  12. Aadhaar, under ECI's 9 Sep 2025 letter
  13. Extract of the Bihar SIR roll (1 July 2025)
Hearing, decision and appeal
  • No deletion without an ERO enquiry and a fair chance to respond.
  • The ERO gives a reasoned, speaking order.
  • First appeal to the District Magistrate, second appeal to the Chief Electoral Officer within 30 days. The Delhi FAQ gives 15 days for the first appeal.
  • Staff must not harass the old, sick, poor or disabled.
Documents at home, hearings and camps (ECI press note, 26 Sep 2026)
  • For a notice about being unmapped or having a logical discrepancy, the BLO visits the home, collects the documents and uploads them on ECINet for the ERO to decide.
  • The elector need not attend the ERO office. Only in exceptional cases the ERO holds a hearing, preferably online.
  • Any adult family member can be authorised to attend a hearing on the elector's behalf.
  • DEOs set up help desks and special camps for night shelters, labour, poor and homeless people.
  • The Form 6 declaration used in SIR has been upheld by the Supreme Court. Outside SIR, the forms in the 1960 Rules apply.
  • Anyone left out, including first-time voters, can apply to the ERO under continuous updation.
  • A committee headed by a Senior Deputy Election Commissioner with an independent IIT or IIIT expert will review ECINet for compliance with the Acts and Rules.
  • Names under the unmapped and logical discrepancy categories must be displayed publicly (ECI directions for West Bengal, Jan 2026).
  • If an application is rejected, the specific reason must be stated.
Support and staffing
  • Party booth-level agents and legal-aid para-legal volunteers help electors file claims.
  • No DEO, ERO or AERO post may stay vacant, and no SIR official is transferred without ECI approval.
Schedule
  • Phase II template: preparation 28 Oct to 3 Nov, enumeration 4 Nov to 4 Dec, draft roll 9 Dec, claims to 8 Jan, notices and decisions to 31 Jan, final roll 7 Feb.
  • Delhi: draft roll 5 Aug 2026. ECI's press note of 26 Sep 2026 extended claims and objections to 30 Oct 2026 and disposal of notices and claims to 30 Nov 2026, so earlier FAQ dates are out of date.
  • Maharashtra: claims to 12 Oct 2026 and disposal to 10 Nov 2026.

Search a scanned SIR roll PDF

Styled after ECI's own "Search in last SIR E-Roll" tool, but it runs OCR (optical character recognition) on your own file in your browser instead of a government database. Many published rolls are scanned images, so ordinary PDF text search does not find a name in them. Nothing is uploaded: the file stays on your device. The first use needs an internet connection, to load the OCR library. Official tool for comparison: voters.eci.gov.in.

Step 2: search

Search also works in the state's native language: type the name in that script. Spelling may differ between the last SIR roll and current records, so if you find nothing, try variations of the name (for example, Maneesh and Manish).
